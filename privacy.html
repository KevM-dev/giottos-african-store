★ Plain English

Your privacy.

This is a small shop website. It has no accounts, no basket and no payments, and we do not run any advertising or analytics tracking on it.

Last updated 26 August 2026

The short version

We do not collect your personal details through this website. There is nowhere on it to create an account or enter a payment. The shopping list you can build stays on your own phone or computer, and we never see it unless you choose to send it to us on WhatsApp.

Your shopping list

When you tap Add to list on a product, your browser saves that choice on your own device using a feature called local storage. It is not a cookie, it is not sent to us, and it is not sent anywhere else. It exists so your list survives closing the tab or walking to the shop.

It stays until you clear it. You can remove it at any time by opening your list and choosing Clear list, or by clearing site data for this website in your browser settings.

When you message us

Sending your list, or using the contact form on the home page, opens WhatsApp with a message already written out. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp itself. At that point your message and phone number go to us through WhatsApp, and WhatsApp is run by Meta, whose own privacy terms apply to that conversation.

We keep those conversations to answer you and to put items by. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not pass your number to anyone else.

Other companies involved in this website

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Their servers keep standard access logs, which include visitors' IP addresses.
  • Google Maps provides the map on the Visit page. When that map loads, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies on your device. If you would rather it did not, block third-party cookies in your browser, or use the plain Open in Google Maps link instead.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking on this website.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies of our own. The only cookies that may appear come from the embedded Google map described above.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk if you are unhappy with how we have handled it.

In practice, the only personal information we are likely to hold is a WhatsApp or email conversation you started. Ask us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change how the website handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top of it.
