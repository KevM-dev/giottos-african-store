★ Plain English

Your privacy.

This is a small shop website. There are no accounts, and no payment is ever taken on the site itself. You can build a shopping list, which stays on your own device, and send it to us on WhatsApp if you choose to. Paying is arranged with us directly afterwards. We do not run any advertising or analytics tracking on this site.

Last updated 9 September 2026

The short version

We do not collect your personal details through this website. There is nowhere on it to create an account or enter a payment. The shopping list you can build stays on your own phone or computer, and we never see it unless you choose to send it to us on WhatsApp.

Who we are

Giottos African Store is the data controller for everything described on this page, which is the legal way of saying we are the ones responsible for your information and the ones to ask about it. You will find us at 355B Aylsham Road, Norwich NR3 2RX, and at 5/6 Middle Market Road, Great Yarmouth NR30 2DT.

For anything about this page, email hello@giottos.co.uk or call us on the shop number.

Why we are allowed to hold this, and for how long

Our lawful basis is legitimate interest: we keep what you send us so we can answer you and put your order by for collection. If you would rather we did not, you have the right to object — say so and we will stop and delete what we hold.

We keep WhatsApp and email conversations for twelve months and then delete them. We do not build a mailing list out of them, and we do not pass your number on to anyone else.

What this website keeps on your device

Two things, and both are saved by your own browser using a feature called local storage. Neither is a cookie, neither is sent to us, and neither is sent anywhere else.

  • Your shopping list. When you tap Add to list on a product, that choice is saved on your phone or computer, so the list survives closing the tab or walking to the shop.
  • Which shop you chose. Once you have picked Norwich or Great Yarmouth, we remember it, so you are not asked again every time you send us something.

Both stay until you clear them. You can empty the list at any time by opening it and choosing Clear list, and you can remove both by clearing site data for this website in your browser settings.

Both are strictly necessary to provide the thing you asked for — a list you can build and send to the right shop. Storage like that is exempt under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, so we do not have to ask your consent for it, and that is why you are not made to click through a banner to use this site.

When you message us

Sending your list, or using the contact form on the home page, opens WhatsApp with a message already written out. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp itself. At that point your message and phone number go to us through WhatsApp, and WhatsApp is run by Meta, whose own privacy terms apply to that conversation.

We keep those conversations to answer you and to put items by. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not pass your number to anyone else.

If you pay by bank transfer, we send you our bank details on WhatsApp and your name shows up on the payment when it reaches us. We never ask for card numbers, and this website has no way to take a card payment — so if anything ever asks you for card details in our name, it is not us.

Other companies involved in this website

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Their servers keep standard access logs, which include visitors' IP addresses.
  • Google Maps provides the maps on the Visit page. They do not load until you press Show map. Once you do, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies on your device. If you would rather it did not, leave them alone and use the plain Open in Google Maps link instead.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking on this website.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies of our own, and none are set when you arrive. The only cookies that may appear come from a Google map, and only after you have pressed Show map on the Visit page. That is why this site does not put a cookie banner in your way.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk if you are unhappy with how we have handled it.

In practice, the only personal information we are likely to hold is a WhatsApp or email conversation you started. Ask us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change how the website handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top of it.
